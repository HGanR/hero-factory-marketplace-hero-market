# Parent Company Setup: Client Positioning Cheat Sheet

**For Consultants: Real Client Conversations**  
**Phase 1 Research: Observe What Works**  

---

## Core Positioning Framework

### Opening Line (Test This)
**"I've been working with a new organizational tool that helps us prepare draft governance documents for your operating company. It creates a structured foundation that makes the legal review process much cleaner."**

### Key: Focus on Process Improvement, Not Automation

**Say This:**
- "Faster preparation of consistent documents"
- "Clear handoff to your legal counsel"
- "Audit trail for decision-making"
- "Banking-ready governance foundation"

**Don't Say This:**
- "Automated compliance"
- "Legal document generation"
- "Filing preparation"
- "Regulatory shortcuts"

---

## Client Conversation Scripts

### When Client Asks "What Does This Do?"

**Response:** *"It takes the information we discuss about your company structure and generates draft bylaws, resolutions, and organizational documents. Think of it as a structured template system that ensures consistency and completeness before your attorney reviews everything."*

**Observation:** Do they ask about filing? Cost savings? Speed?

### When Client Asks "Is This Legal Advice?"

**Response:** *"No, this is organizational assistance only. All outputs are drafts that require review and adoption by your legal counsel and board. We're helping prepare materials, not providing legal services."*

**Observation:** Do they accept this boundary? Do they push for more?

### When Client Asks "How Long Does It Take?"

**Response:** *"The draft preparation typically takes 30-45 minutes once we have your company details. The real time investment comes in the professional review and formal adoption process that follows."*

**Observation:** Do they focus on the draft time or total process?

### When Client Asks "What About Banking/Compliance?"

**Response:** *"The system includes banking-focused modules that ensure your governance documents meet typical counterparty expectations. However, all documents still need final review by your legal team and formal adoption by your board."*

**Observation:** Do they ask about specific bank requirements? Accept the review requirement?

---

## Handling Common Objections

### Objection: "I Just Need Basic Documents"

**Response:** *"This gives you more than basic documents - it provides professionally structured drafts with proper governance frameworks. Many clients find the comprehensive approach saves time during legal review because fewer revisions are needed."*

**Observation:** Do they still want "basic"? What do they consider "basic"?

### Objection: "My Attorney Handles All Documents"

**Response:** *"Perfect - this tool creates the draft foundation your attorney can build upon. It ensures we provide them with complete, well-organized information from the start."*

**Observation:** Do they see this as helpful handoff or redundant?

### Objection: "This Sounds Like Legal Work"

**Response:** *"We're staying firmly in the organizational planning space. This is about gathering information and creating structured drafts - the legal analysis and final documents remain with licensed professionals."*

**Observation:** Does this reassurance work? Do they continue to express concern?

## Process Walkthrough Language

### Setting Expectations
**"We'll work through this together in about 30 minutes. You'll see draft documents at the end, but remember these are starting points for your legal team's review and formal adoption."**

### During the Wizard
**"This system asks structured questions to ensure we capture all the key governance details. It's like a comprehensive checklist that results in draft documents."**

### At Document Generation
**"Here are the draft documents based on what we've discussed. Each one includes clear markings that it's a draft requiring professional review. We'll share these with your legal counsel for their input."**

### Discussing Next Steps
**"The drafts give us and your legal team a solid foundation. From here, it's the standard process of legal review, board approval, and formal execution."**

## Emergency Language (If Needed)

### If Client Demands Filing Services
**"I'm sorry, but we cannot provide filing services through this platform. All regulatory filings must be handled through licensed professionals and proper state agencies. We can certainly help prepare the organizational foundation for that process."**

### If Client Wants Legal Opinions
**"We cannot provide legal advice or opinions. This tool helps organize information for your legal counsel, but all legal analysis and recommendations must come from licensed attorneys."**

### If Scope Creep Occurs
**"To maintain quality and compliance, we keep this tool focused on draft preparation. For any legal services or filings, we'll connect you with appropriate professionals."
