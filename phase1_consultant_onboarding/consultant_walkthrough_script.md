# Parent Company + C-Corp Setup Wizard
## Consultant Onboarding Walkthrough Script

**Phase 1: Controlled Live Usage**  
**Duration**: 30 minutes  
**Purpose**: Behavioral validation and real-world usage observation  

---

## Walkthrough Overview

### Target Audience
- Trust and estate consultants
- Corporate formation specialists
- Family office advisors

### Session Structure
- **15 minutes**: Platform walkthrough and positioning
- **10 minutes**: Hands-on wizard demonstration
- **5 minutes**: Q&A and next steps

### Key Behavioral Observations to Track
*(For research purposes - do not interrupt the walkthrough)*
- Where consultant pauses or backtracks
- Which explanations they rephrase
- Which features they skip or rush
- What questions they ask about scope/limitations

---

## Section 1: Platform Positioning (10 minutes)

### Opening Script

**Consultant:** *"Today we're walking through a new tool that helps us prepare draft governance documents for operating companies. This is designed as a structured preparation layer that improves our handoff quality to legal counsel and accountants."*

**Show homepage and navigate to Family Office → "Parent Company / C-Corp"**

### Key Positioning Points

#### What It Is
*"This platform generates draft governance and organizational documents based on information you provide. Think of it as a structured preparation assistant that creates consistent, review-grade materials."*

#### What It Isn't
*"This is not a legal service, filing platform, or compliance automation tool. All outputs require professional review and formal adoption before use."*

#### Value Proposition
*"It reduces drafting errors, creates audit trails, and ensures we hand off clean packages to your legal team. The result is faster, more consistent service delivery."*

### Behavioral Observation Points
- [ ] Does consultant accept positioning without questions?
- [ ] Do they rephrase any explanations?
- [ ] What analogies do they use when explaining to themselves?

---

## Section 2: Wizard Demonstration (15 minutes)

### Step 1: Structure Builder
**Navigate to `/wizard?type=company`**

**Consultant:** *"The wizard starts with a Structure Builder that pre-configures the system based on your intended corporate architecture."*

**Demonstrate:**
1. Select "Parent Holding + Operating Sub"
2. Show how modules auto-populate
3. Explain preset logic

**Key Script:** *"This reduces decision fatigue by asking one question up front, then configuring everything else automatically."*

**Observation:** Time to complete this step, any hesitation on structure choices.

### Step 2: Setup Details
**Complete basic company information**

**Consultant:** *"Now we capture the foundational details. Notice how the system enforces state-aware defaults."*

**Demonstrate:**
1. Enter company name and state
2. Show entity type selection
3. Explain public ID generation

**Key Script:** *"The system generates a unique public ID (like COMP-DE-2026-0001) for tracking and audit purposes."*

**Observation:** Which fields cause hesitation, questions about ID format.

### Step 3: Onboarding Modules
**Select and explain modules**

**Consultant:** *"Based on your structure choice, certain modules are pre-selected. You can customize, but this gives you a banking-ready foundation."*

**Highlight key modules:**
- Formation (authorized shares, par value)
- Governance (board size, officer roles)
- Equity (founder shares, option pools)
- Banking (signatory rules, EIN planning)

**Key Script:** *"The banking module is particularly valuable - it ensures your governance documents meet counterparty expectations."*

**Observation:** Which modules they question or skip.

### Step 4: Generate Outputs
**Complete remaining steps and generate documents**

**Consultant:** *"The system now generates draft documents with proper watermarks and disclaimers. Each document is versioned and can be regenerated if needed."*

**Show:**
1. Document generation process
2. Draft watermarks and footers
3. Version history

**Key Script:** *"All outputs are clearly marked as drafts requiring professional review. This protects both us and our clients."*

**Observation:** Which documents they download, questions about formatting.

### Step 5: Execution Readiness
**Demonstrate status workflow**

**Consultant:** *"The platform includes an execution readiness workflow that tracks document lifecycle from draft through formal adoption."*

**Show status progression:**
- Draft → Counsel Reviewed → Board Adopted → Execution Ready

**Key Script:** *"This creates a clear audit trail and ensures nothing moves forward without proper professional involvement."*

**Observation:** Understanding of workflow, questions about state meanings.

---

## Section 3: Q&A and Next Steps (5 minutes)

### Handle Common Questions

#### "Can this file the documents?"
**Response:** *"No, this is explicitly a draft-generation tool only. All filings must go through proper legal channels."*

#### "Is this legal advice?"
**Response:** *"Absolutely not. This is organizational assistance that improves handoff to licensed professionals."*

#### "What if a client wants more automation?"
**Response:** *"We maintain strict boundaries. If a client needs legal services, we facilitate that handoff professionally."*

### Close and Next Steps

**Consultant:** *"Thank you for walking through. In your first engagement, focus on observing where the flow feels natural and where it doesn't. We'll use that feedback to refine the experience."*

**Provide:**
- Consultant Playbook PDF
- Access credentials for real client work
- Feedback collection form
