## Purpose and Scope

This playbook provides operational guidelines for consultants using the platform to prepare draft governance and organizational documents for clients. It emphasizes risk management, appropriate positioning, and professional boundaries.

## How to Position the Platform

### Approved Language and Descriptions

**Use these descriptions:**

> "A system that helps us organize your structure, prepare draft governance documents, and create a clean record for counsel, banks, and counterparties."

> "Draft governance materials prepared for professional review and formal adoption."

> "Structured preparation tools that improve handoff quality to your legal and tax professionals."

**Emphasize these benefits:**
- Faster draft preparation
- Consistent document organization
- Clear audit trails
- Professional workflow integration

### Prohibited Language

**Do not describe the platform as:**

- "Entity formation service"
- "Legal document preparation"
- "Tax planning tool"
- "Compliance automation"
- "Regulatory filing system"
- "Document execution platform"

**Avoid these claims:**
- "Automatically compliant"
- "Legally sufficient"
- "Tax-optimized"
- "Bank-approved"

## Best Engagement Flow

### Phase 1: Client Intake and Assessment

1. **Identify client objectives**
   - What entities need to be established?
   - What governance structures are required?
   - What are the operational and compliance goals?

2. **Assess complexity and scope**
   - Determine appropriate presets and modules
   - Identify required professional involvement
   - Set expectations for timeline and deliverables

3. **Explain platform role**
   - Position as "draft preparation assistant"
   - Emphasize professional review requirements
   - Discuss handoff to legal counsel

### Phase 2: Platform Usage

1. **Select appropriate preset**
   - Use "Standard Delaware C-Corp" for typical operating companies
   - Use "Holding Company + Operating Sub" for complex structures
   - Use "Custom" only when client requirements are unusual

2. **Complete wizard systematically**
   - Structure Builder → Setup → Onboarding → Selected Modules → Review
   - Validate information at each step
   - Save drafts frequently

3. **Generate outputs strategically**
   - Focus on governance documents first
   - Generate equity materials after legal review
   - Use version control for iterative improvements

### Phase 3: Professional Handoff and Execution

1. **Prepare for counsel review**
   - Export draft documents with version history
   - Provide context about client objectives
   - Highlight areas requiring legal customization

2. **Coordinate execution process**
   - Schedule board meetings for formal adoption
   - Prepare execution packages
   - Track completion status

3. **Maintain records**
   - Store finalized versions externally
   - Keep draft history in-platform for reference
   - Document professional consultations

## Risk Management Protocols

### Document Status Communication

**Always communicate that documents are:**
- "Prepared drafts requiring professional review"
- "Not filed or executed instruments"
- "Subject to legal counsel approval"
- "For internal governance preparation only"

**Never present documents as:**
- "Final and approved"
- "Ready for filing"
- "Legally sufficient"
- "Compliance certified"

### Client Education Requirements

**Before platform use, ensure clients understand:**
1. Platform generates drafts, not final documents
2. Professional review is mandatory
3. Execution requires formal adoption
4. Regulatory filings are separate processes

**Document client acknowledgment:**
- Use written confirmations
- Include in engagement letters
- Reference in meeting notes

### Professional Boundaries

**Consultants must:**
- Never provide legal opinions through the platform
- Never bypass required professional reviews
- Never represent platform outputs as complete solutions
- Always disclose platform-generated status to counterparties

**Consultants should:**
- Coordinate with legal counsel throughout process
- Verify all outputs meet client objectives
- Document all professional consultations
- Maintain clear audit trails

## Module Selection Guidelines

### Formation Module
- **Required for**: All new entities
- **Purpose**: Capture basic organizational details
- **Review needs**: State-specific requirements, registered agent

### Governance Module
- **Required for**: All corporate entities
- **Purpose**: Board structure, officer roles, initial consents
- **Review needs**: Fiduciary duties, authority delegations

### Equity Module
- **Required for**: Companies with founders or investors
- **Purpose**: Founder equity, option pools, vesting
- **Review needs**: Securities law compliance, tax implications

### Banking Module
- **Recommended for**: Companies needing bank accounts
- **Purpose**: Signatory rules, account opening readiness
- **Review needs**: Bank-specific requirements, authority documentation

### Subsidiaries Module
- **Required for**: Holding company structures
- **Purpose**: Parent-subsidiary relationships
- **Review needs**: Intercompany agreements, governance alignment

### IP Module
- **Recommended for**: Companies with intellectual property
- **Purpose**: IP assignment, invention policies
- **Review needs**: Employment agreements, contractor arrangements

### Compliance Module
- **Required for**: All entities
- **Purpose**: Annual reporting, franchise tax awareness
- **Review needs**: Jurisdiction-specific obligations

## Incident Response

### If Client Misunderstands Platform Role

1. **Immediately clarify** draft-only status
2. **Document clarification** in writing
3. **Escalate to supervising consultant** if needed
4. **Consider engagement pause** until understanding confirmed

### If Counterparty Questions Document Status

1. **Provide bank explanation sheet** immediately
2. **Direct counterparty to final executed versions**
3. **Offer to facilitate professional verification**
4. **Document all communications**

### If Regulatory or Compliance Concerns Arise

1. **Stop all platform use** for affected client
2. **Consult legal counsel** immediately
3. **Preserve all records** and audit trails
4. **Report incident** per firm policies
